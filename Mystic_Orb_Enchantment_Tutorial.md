# 🔮 Mystic Orb & Weapon Enchantment System Tutorial

Welcome to the ultimate guide for using the Mystic Orb enchantment system! This mod allows you to permanently enhance your weapons with magical power, making them stronger in combat.

## 📋 What You Need to Know

The enchantment system has two main components:
1. **Mystic Orbs** - Magical items that bind to weapons and provide permanent damage bonuses
2. **Weapon Enchanting** - A point-based system that lets you enhance weapons with various effects

---

## 🔮 Part 1: Mystic Orb System

### What is a Mystic Orb?

A Mystic Orb is a special magical item that looks like a battery in your inventory. When you bind it to a weapon, it permanently increases that weapon's damage by **+3 points** to both minimum and maximum damage.

### How to Use Mystic Orbs

#### Step 1: Find or Craft a Mystic Orb
- Mystic Orbs can be found as loot from TANK ZED
- They appear as "Mystic Orb Weapon" in your inventory
- They're lightweight (0.1 weight) so they won't burden you

#### Step 2: Bind the Orb to Your Weapon
1. **Equip the weapon** you want to enhance in your hands
2. **Right-click on the Mystic Orb** in your inventory
3. Select **"Bind it to my weapon"** from the context menu
4. The orb will disappear from your inventory (it's consumed in the process)
5. Your character will say something to confirm the binding

#### Step 3: Enjoy Your Enhanced Weapon
- Your weapon now deals **+3 extra damage** on every hit
- The weapon's name will show **(Enhanced)** to indicate it's been upgraded
- This enhancement is **permanent** and stays with the weapon forever

### Important Notes About Mystic Orbs

- ✅ **One orb per weapon** - Each weapon can only be bound to one orb
- ✅ **One weapon per orb** - Each orb can only be used once
- ✅ **Permanent effect** - The enhancement never wears off
- ✅ **Works on any weapon** - Melee weapons, firearms, etc.
- ❌ **Cannot be undone** - Once bound, you cannot remove the orb

### Checking Orb Binding Status

To check if a weapon is bound to a mystic orb:
1. **Hold the weapon** in your hands
2. **Right-click** on the weapon or in your inventory
3. Select **"Check Orb Binding"**
4. Your character will tell you if the weapon is enhanced or not

## ⚡ Part 2: Weapon Enchantment System

### What is Weapon Enchanting?

The enchantment system lets you spend points to randomly improve or worsen your weapons. It's a gambling system where you can get powerful bonuses or unfortunate penalties.

### How Enchanting Works

#### Step 1: Open the Enchantment UI
- Use the command or interface to open the enchanting window
- You'll see your current weapon and enchantment options

#### Step 2: Choose Your Options

**Basic Enchantment (2,500 points):**
- Costs 2,500 points per attempt
- Can result in positive or negative effects
- If you get a negative effect, your weapon might break!

**Protected Enchantment (12,500 points):**
- Costs 12,500 points per attempt (10,000 extra for protection)
- Check the "Enable Enchant Protection" box
- Your weapon won't break even if you get negative effects
- Much safer but more expensive

#### Step 3: Apply the Enchantment
1. Make sure you have enough points
2. Click the enchant button
3. The system will randomly determine the effect
4. Your weapon will be renamed to show its enchantment level

### Understanding Enchantment Results

**Positive Enchantments (+1 to +10):**
- Your weapon becomes stronger
- Weapon name shows: `WeaponName_YourUsername_+1` (example)
- Each positive level improves the weapon

**Negative Enchantments (-1 to -10):**
- Your weapon becomes weaker
- Weapon name shows: `WeaponName_YourUsername_-1` (example)
- Without protection, negative enchantments can break your weapon!

**Enchantment Limits:**
- Maximum positive enchantment: **+10**
- Maximum negative enchantment: **-10**
- You cannot enchant beyond these limits

### Enchantment Strategy Tips

#### For Beginners
- Start with **protected enchantments** to avoid losing weapons
- Use cheaper weapons first to practice
- Save your best weapons until you understand the system

#### For Risk-Takers
- Unprotected enchantments are cheaper but dangerous
- Good for weapons you don't mind losing
- Higher risk, but you can enchant more often

#### Smart Approach
- Use **Mystic Orbs first** for guaranteed +3 damage
- Then use **protected enchantments** to safely try for more bonuses
- Keep backup weapons in case something goes wrong

## 🎯 Combining Both Systems

### The Ultimate Enhancement Strategy

1. **Find a good weapon** you want to keep long-term
2. **Bind a Mystic Orb** to it for guaranteed +3 damage
3. **Use protected enchantments** to safely try for additional bonuses
4. **Keep trying** until you reach +10 or run out of points

### Example Enhancement Path

Let's say you have a baseball bat:
- **Original damage:** 1.2 - 1.8
- **After Mystic Orb:** 4.2 - 4.8 (Enhanced)
- **After +5 enchantment:** Even higher damage + renamed to "Base.Bat_YourName_+5"

## ⚠️ Important Safety Tips

### Before You Start
- **Backup your save** if you're worried about losing items
- **Practice on cheap weapons** before using on valuable ones
- **Have multiple weapons** ready in case one breaks

### Common Mistakes to Avoid
- Don't enchant without protection unless you're okay with losing the weapon
- Don't waste orbs on weapons you might replace soon
- Don't forget to equip the weapon before trying to bind an orb

### Troubleshooting

**"Already Bound" message:**
- The orb has already been used on another weapon
- Each orb can only be used once

**"You need to hold a weapon" message:**
- Make sure the weapon is in your hands, not just in inventory
- The weapon must be a valid weapon type

**Enchantment fails:**
- Check you have enough points
- Make sure you're holding a weapon
- The weapon might already be at maximum enchantment level
